Research experience
------

**DPhil research, University of Oxford** (Oct 2022 – Jul 2026)

*Neural mechanisms of sensorimotor control of speech*

* Designed and ran a preregistered brain stimulation study (90 participants) testing whether tDCS over motor cortex and cerebellum enhances speech adaptation. The published effect did not replicate; the result appeared in *Cortex* (2025) with [open data and code](https://osf.io/9bswe/).
* Designed and led a behavioural study (76 participants) and a functional and structural MRI study (48 participants) on the cortical and cerebellar mechanisms of speech adaptation under altered auditory feedback; manuscripts in preparation.
* Initiated and coordinated a two-site collaboration between the University of Oxford and Beijing Normal University, running matched behavioural and fMRI protocols in the UK and China.

*Altered brain structure and function in people who stutter*

* Analysed a large neuroimaging dataset on developmental stuttering (~300 participants) using structural and resting-state fMRI; manuscript in preparation.
* Built reusable, version-controlled analysis pipelines (FSL, SPM, MATLAB, Python) now used by others in the group.

**Master's research, Beijing Normal University** (Sep 2019 – Jun 2022)

*Role of the cerebellum in speech, language and reading*

* Examined the dissociation between language control and domain-general cognitive control within the cerebellum, combining an fMRI study (48 participants) with a tDCS study (72 participants) and effective-connectivity modelling of cerebello-cortical circuits ([*Cerebral Cortex*, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhac051)).
* Proposed the cerebro-cerebellar mapping hypothesis of the cerebellum's contribution to reading, in a systematic review (*Neuropsychologia*, 2022).

*Neurobiology of bilingualism*

* Mapped the cortical networks of bilingual language production across two fMRI studies (~50 participants each) using multivoxel pattern analysis and effective-connectivity network modelling ([*Brain Structure and Function*, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00429-021-02218-7)).
* Ran a [preregistered EEG study](https://osf.io/xyqfr/) (24 participants) on continuous neural tracking of speech in noise in bilinguals ([*Brain and Language*, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bandl.2022.105128)).
* Co-developed an open database of ~3,000 Chinese characters and ~2,200 words from primary-school textbooks, a resource for developmental reading research.
